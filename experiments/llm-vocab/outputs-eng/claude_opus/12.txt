**Proposal: Structured Logging for the Payment Service**

**Problem:** The payment service currently emits minimal, unstructured logs, making it difficult to trace failed transactions, diagnose gateway timeouts, or reconstruct events during incident reviews and audits.

**Proposal:** Introduce structured JSON logging across the payment service with a consistent schema: timestamp, correlation ID, payment ID, user ID (hashed), event type, status, latency, and error details. Log at each critical boundary — request received, gateway call, gateway response, and final outcome.

**Requirements:**
- Never log card numbers, CVVs, or full PII; enforce redaction at the logger layer.
- Propagate correlation IDs from upstream services for end-to-end tracing.
- Ship logs to our central aggregator with 90-day retention for compliance.

**Effort:** Approximately one sprint — logging library integration, redaction middleware, and updates to the four main handlers.

**Benefits:** Faster incident resolution, clearer audit trails, and the foundation for alerting on failure-rate spikes.
