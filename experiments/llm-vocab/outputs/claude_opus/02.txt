**Solid Oak Desk Lamp**

Warm light, honest materials. This desk lamp is turned from solid oak and finished with a hand-rubbed natural oil that brings out the grain instead of hiding it — so no two lamps look quite alike. The articulated arm pivots smoothly and holds its position without drooping, letting you throw light exactly where you need it: across a keyboard, onto a sketchpad, or up the wall for a softer glow.

Inside, a dimmable LED module puts out 450 lumens at 2700K — the color of late-afternoon sun, easy on the eyes through a long evening. Touch the brass collar to cycle brightness through three levels. The weighted base keeps everything steady, even at full extension.

Measures 16" tall with a 14" reach. Uses 9W, rated for 25,000 hours, and the module is replaceable rather than disposable.

Built to sit on a desk you actually use, for years.
