Congratulations on your first job. Here's what I'd tell you:

**Ask questions early.** Nobody expects you to know everything; they expect you to learn. The window when "I'm new" is a perfectly good reason to ask closes faster than you'd think, so use it.

**Be reliable before you try to be impressive.** Show up on time, do what you said you'd do, and communicate when you can't. Trust is built on small, boring consistency, and it compounds.

**Write things down.** Names, decisions, how systems work, feedback you receive. Your memory will fail you; your notes won't.

**Find people to learn from, not just a boss.** Watch how the best colleagues handle problems and disagreements. Ask them to coffee.

**Take feedback seriously but not personally.** Criticism of your work is data, not a verdict on you.

**Don't compare your beginning to someone else's middle.** Everyone was new once. Give yourself time.
