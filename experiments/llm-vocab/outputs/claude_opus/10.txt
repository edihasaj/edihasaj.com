My ideal Sunday starts slow. No alarm—just waking whenever the light through the window decides it's time. Coffee first, made properly, drunk on the balcony while the city is still quiet and nobody needs anything from me yet.

Late morning, a long walk somewhere green. No podcast, no headphones. Just noticing things: the way shadows fall, a dog being ridiculous, the smell of someone's bakery. Walking is where thoughts untangle themselves without being forced.

Afternoon is for a small, unhurried project. Not work—something with no deadline. Maybe a side experiment I've been curious about, maybe a book I've been putting off, maybe cooking something that takes three hours for no good reason.

Evening: dinner with people I actually like, conversation that wanders wherever it wants. Then an early-ish night, a little tired in the good way, with the pleasant sense that nothing was optimized and nothing needed to be.
