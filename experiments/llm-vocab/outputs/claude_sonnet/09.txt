Starting a new job can feel overwhelming, but a few habits make the transition much smoother:

1. **Listen more than you talk** early on. Spend your first weeks learning how things actually work before pushing to change them.

2. **Ask questions**—good ones. Nobody expects you to know everything, but they do expect curiosity and follow-through.

3. **Build relationships**, not just with your manager, but with peers across teams. They'll teach you more than any onboarding doc.

4. **Take notes obsessively.** You'll be given a lot of information fast; writing it down shows diligence and saves you from repeating questions.

5. **Underpromise, overdeliver.** Reliability builds trust faster than flashy work.

6. **Take initiative once you understand the lay of the land**—volunteer for tasks, but don't overstep before you know the culture.

7. **Be patient with yourself.** Competence and confidence come with time, not instantly.

Above all, show up with humility and a genuine willingness to learn—that combination goes further than raw talent in the first few months.
