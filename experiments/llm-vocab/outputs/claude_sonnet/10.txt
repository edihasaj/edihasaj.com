My ideal Sunday starts slow — no alarm, just natural light and the first coffee made carefully, not rushed. I'd spend the morning outside: a long walk or an easy bike ride somewhere with trees or water, phone mostly ignored. Brunch would involve something simple but good — eggs, good bread, maybe a farmers market stop for fruit I don't actually need but want anyway.

The afternoon is for one unhurried project: reading a book I've been meaning to finish, tinkering with a small side project, or cooking something a little ambitious for dinner. No meetings, no deadlines pressing in the background.

Evening means people — a relaxed dinner with friends or family, conversation that wanders, maybe a movie or just sitting outside as it gets dark. I'd end the night early, not doomscrolling, just winding down with music or a few pages of a book, already looking forward to Monday instead of dreading it.
