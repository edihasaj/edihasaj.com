Hi Edi — here’s to a Sunday that feels spacious.

My ideal Sunday begins slowly, with pale morning light, strong coffee, and nowhere urgent to be. I’d read by an open window while the city gradually wakes, then take a long walk through quiet streets or along a wooded trail. Lunch would be simple and generous: fresh bread, eggs, tomatoes, good cheese, perhaps shared with someone who makes conversation effortless.

The afternoon would hold a small creative project—writing, sketching, building something unnecessary but satisfying—followed by music and a nap without an alarm. Later, I’d cook dinner carefully, preferably something that fills the kitchen with garlic, herbs, and warmth.

As evening settles, I’d put away every screen, light a lamp, and talk, read, or watch an old film. The day would end early, with clean sheets, rain outside if I’m lucky, and Monday still distant enough not to matter.
