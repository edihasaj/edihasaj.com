Hi Edi — a slow Sunday resets the whole week.

My ideal Sunday starts before the city fully wakes: coffee, open window, no notifications. I’d read something absorbing for an hour—essay, history, a strange novel—then take a long walk somewhere green, ideally with a bakery stop folded into the route.

The afternoon would be lightly social: cooking lunch with friends or family, food that takes time but not precision, plenty of conversation drifting between plans and nonsense. A few hours for making something small follows—writing, designing, tinkering with code—without the pressure to finish.

By evening, I’d want the house quiet again. A simple dinner, a film worth paying attention to, then a short review of the coming week: enough structure to make Monday feel clear, not crowded. Early sleep, phone in another room.
